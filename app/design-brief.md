# Octo — Design Brief

## Design read
A premium AI company that makes intelligence feel inevitable. Dark, stark, precise. The site should feel like a high-end research lab or a luxury watchmaker, not a startup pitch deck.

## Concept spine
The site is a dark room with a single light source. Intelligence emerges from shadow, detail by detail, until the whole picture is clear.

## Delivery tier
cinema — Lenis + GSAP, Tier-1 scroll-scrub journey, section chapters below the journey.

## Locked palette
- Background: #080808 (near-void black)
- Ink: #f0f0f0 (off-white, not pure white)
- Accent: #d4d4d4 (silver — used ONLY on the scroll progress bar and one CTA detail)
- Muted: #666666 (warm mid-gray for secondary text)
No other colors. No gradients on large surfaces. No glows. Strict monochrome.

## Locked type
- Display: Geist (sans-serif, technical precision)
- Mono: Geist Mono (data readouts, labels)
- Rationale: Octo is a systems/AI company; grotesque geometric = correct register. Serif would read as heritage/luxury, which misrepresents the brand.

## Animation mode
animated-website — scroll-scrub single-shot, ~15s continuous film.

### Journey (single-shot, 1 film, 5 chapters mapped to film moments)

**World grammar:** Stark monochrome. Deep black ground (#080808), luminous white subject (#f0f0f0), silver glow from within. One continuous slow orbit + push-in camera move. No cuts, no camera shake. Slow steady motion throughout.

**Film prompt (seedance_2_0, style reference from storyboard):**
Abstract AI intelligence visualization — a luminous multi-armed tendril form emerging from pure darkness, slowly rotating, arms unfurling and reconnecting like a neural network or mycelium made of light. Studio black background. Monochrome: pure white luminous form against deep black. Slow, steady 360-degree orbit camera. No cuts. No camera shake. Slow, constant motion. Clean sharp focus throughout. No text, no logos, no watermarks.

**Chapters (mapped to film moments 0–15s):**
1. 0–3s: Emergence — a single point of light in darkness — "Intelligence begins in the dark."
2. 3–6s: Expansion — tendrils unfurl — "Patterns emerge from noise."
3. 6–9s: Connection — nodes link, form activates — "Octo connects the dots."
4. 9–12s: Clarity — full form revealed — "From complexity, precision."
5. 12–15s: Horizon — camera settles, form complete — "The future is already here."
